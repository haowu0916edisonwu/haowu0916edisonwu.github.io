I study reliable and efficient foundation models, particularly uncertainty quantification, adaptive inference, and multimodal reasoning. My work examines how models evaluate evidence, allocate inference-time computation, and improve prediction and generation across language, vision, and scientific tasks. I am an undergraduate researcher at UW–Madison, working with Prof. Xiao Luo (UW–Madison) and Prof. Hang Zhou (UNC–Chapel Hill).

我研究可靠且高效的基础模型，重点关注不确定性量化、自适应推理与多模态推理。我探索模型如何评估证据、分配推理阶段的计算资源，以及如何改进语言、视觉与科学任务中的预测和生成。目前在威斯康星大学麦迪逊分校从事本科科研，接受该校罗潇教授与北卡罗来纳大学教堂山分校周航教授的指导。

Research

研究方向

01 · RELIABILITY & UNCERTAINTY
01 · 可靠性与不确定性
Uncertainty Evaluation and Robust Generation
不确定性评估与鲁棒生成

RecUQ evaluates uncertainty monitors for fixed LLM rankings and distinguishes failure detection from responses to controlled evidence changes. GOING studies confidence-guided response selection when retrieved information can mislead generation.

RecUQ 在固定的 LLM 排序结果上评价不确定性信号，区分错误检测与对受控证据变化的响应；GOING 研究外部检索信息可能误导生成时，如何根据置信度选择回答。

02 · EFFICIENT & ADAPTIVE INFERENCE
02 · 高效与自适应推理
Diffusion Refinement and Budgeted Reasoning
扩散模型修正与预算约束推理

CaRR improves low-budget diffusion-language-model refinement through token remasking and trajectory-based output recovery. ORDER combines opening-based cost forecasting, shared-budget scheduling, and multi-student answer probing for efficient reasoning.

CaRR 通过 Token Remasking 和基于轨迹的答案恢复改善低预算扩散语言模型推理；ORDER 结合初始推理阶段的成本预测、共享预算调度和多学生答案探测，实现更高效的推理。

03 · MULTIMODAL EVIDENCE
03 · 多模态证据
Online Video Reasoning and Medical Imaging
在线视频推理与医疗影像

CENTRE addresses what video evidence to retain and when to respond, using multi-resolution context and calibrated response triggering. MARIE investigates evidence grounding for 3D CT report generation.

CENTRE 通过多分辨率上下文和校准的响应触发机制，研究在线视频中保留哪些证据、何时作答；MARIE 研究 3D CT 报告生成中的证据定位。

04 · AI FOR SCIENCE
04 · 科学智能
Molecular Optimization and Test-Time Scaling
分子优化与测试时计算扩展

Our molecular inference benchmark compares nine strategies on two frozen language models across ten tasks. It evaluates twenty ordered outputs per input, separating success coverage from ordering, chemical validity, and generation workload.

分子推理基准在两种冻结语言模型和十项任务上比较九种策略，每个输入评估二十个有序输出，区分成功候选覆盖、输出顺序、分子有效性与生成开销。
